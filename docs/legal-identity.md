# Legal identity and public naming

## Legal container

The planned legal container is ARTJ LLC, a New York single-member LLC owned by
Andy, subject to attorney and CPA confirmation. ARTJ LLC is the intended legal
owner and contracting party for:

- the bank account and EIN;
- federal, New York, and other applicable tax matters;
- contracts and invoices;
- payment processors;
- shared platform code and intellectual property; and
- early experiments that do not yet justify separate operating companies.

“Agent Foundry” is an internal technical/repository name. It should not be
presented as a separate corporation or forced into a public master brand.

## Public naming rule

Use product and shop names when they help customers understand the offering:

- Etsy storefronts: their own shop names.
- Standalone websites: their own names or the product name.
- Individual products: product titles without automatically creating DBAs.

When ARTJ LLC conducts business under a name other than its legal name, review
whether New York requires a Certificate of Assumed Name. A persistent,
meaningful storefront or business name is a DBA candidate; a one-off product
label is not automatically a separate assumed name. This classification must
be confirmed by New York counsel.

## Required disclosure

Default public language:

> © 2026 ARTJ LLC. This website/shop is owned and operated by ARTJ LLC.

Place it in website footers, terms and conditions, invoices, receipts, seller
disclosures, payment-processor business descriptions, and marketplace legal
information fields where available. If a platform limits the text, use a
counsel-approved shortened form such as “Operated by ARTJ LLC.”

## System behavior

The control plane must identify ARTJ LLC as the planned legal container,
distinguish legal names from product, storefront, and repository names, ask
whether persistent non-legal names need assumed-name review, preserve
citations and unresolved questions for counsel/CPA review, and keep formation,
registration, contracting, payment, and public-write tools disabled unless
separately approved and configured.
